A free online diary for girls

The diary at the top of this page is free to use and very simple. Type a title, write your page, and it saves by itself.

  • No sign up. No account, no email, no password to remember.
  • Stays on your device. Your pages are saved in this browser on this device. They are not posted anywhere and are not sent to us.
  • PIN lock. Set a PIN so the diary stays closed if someone else uses the computer or tablet. It is a simple lock, not encryption.
  • Print or save as PDF. Keep your favorite pages in a folder.
  • Works on any device. Use it in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Pages do not sync between devices. If you write on your phone, your pages stay on your phone. Open the full online diary any time, or read about keeping a private diary with a lock.

How to pick a paper diary for girls

A paper diary feels special. If you are choosing one for yourself or as a gift, think about age and how she likes to write.

AgeLook forWhy
6 to 8Wide lines, room to draw, a short prompt on each pageWriting is still new, and pictures help
8 to 11A lock and key, fun cover, undated pagesPrivacy starts to matter a lot
11 to 14Plain or dotted pages, a pocket, an elastic bandRoom for lists, doodles and keepsakes
14 and upA good notebook with thick paper, or a guided journalSpace to write longer and decorate

Things that make a diary easier to keep

  • Undated pages, so missing a day does not leave an empty page.
  • A ribbon bookmark to find today's page fast.
  • Thick paper that works with gel pens and markers.
  • A back pocket for tickets, notes and photos.
  • A size she will carry. A smaller diary fits in a school bag.

A diary lock is mostly a sign that says "private". The real protection is a family agreement that diaries are not read without asking.

How to start your diary

  1. Write the datePut today's date at the top of the page.
  2. Introduce yourselfOn the first page, tell your diary who you are: your name, age, your best friends, your favorite things right now.
  3. Write about todayOne thing that happened and how it made you feel.
  4. Make it yoursAdd a doodle, a sticker or a fun title. Online, give each page a title you like.
  5. Pick a diary timeBedtime works for most people. A few minutes is enough.

Keeping your diary private and safe

  • Keep a paper diary somewhere only you know, or one with a lock.
  • Use a PIN on an online diary if others share the device.
  • Never post diary pages online. Do not share your full name, school, address or photos of yourself with strangers.
  • Talk to a trusted adult if you write about something that is really worrying you or someone is hurting you. A diary is a great place to sort out feelings, but some things need a grown up's help.
  • Ask your family to respect your diary. Most parents are happy to agree that a diary is private.

Why girls love keeping a diary

A diary listens without interrupting. It is a place to work out friend drama, plan big dreams, remember fun days and be completely yourself. Writing about feelings can help them make more sense. And years from now, your diary will be the best way to remember who you were at this age.

Questions people ask

What is a good diary for a girl?

A good diary for a girl matches her age. Younger girls like wide lines, space to draw and a prompt on each page. Girls 8 to 11 often love a lock and key. Teens may prefer a plain notebook with thick paper or a private online diary with a PIN.

What should a girl write in her diary?

She can write about her day, friends, family, feelings, favorite books and bands, goals and dreams. Fun lists, letters to her future self, doodles and stickers all belong too. The diary is hers, so anything that is on her mind is a good thing to write.

Is there a free online diary for girls?

Yes. The diary on this page is free with no sign up and no account. Pages are saved in the browser on your device and are not sent anywhere. You can lock it with a PIN on a shared computer and print any page.

How do I keep my diary secret from my siblings?

Use a diary with a lock, keep it in a hiding place, or use an online diary with a PIN. You can also write in a code or use initials instead of names. It also helps to ask your parents to set a family rule that diaries are private.

What age is a diary with a lock for?

Diaries with a lock and key are most popular with girls from about 7 to 12, when privacy starts to feel important. The lock is mostly for fun and a sign that says private. Older girls often switch to a plain journal or a private online diary.
