Prayer journal

What is a prayer journal?

A prayer journal is a notebook or digital page where you write your prayers to God. It usually holds the date, a Bible verse, praise and thanks, prayers for others, your own requests, and a record of answered prayers. It helps you focus when you pray and remember how God has been faithful over time.

Prayer journal meaning

A prayer journal is simply prayer that you write down. Instead of praying only in your head or out loud, you put your words on paper or on a screen, addressed to God.

Some people write long letters. Others keep short lists. Some follow a method like ACTS. All of them are prayer journals. What makes it a prayer journal is not the format but the purpose: talking with God and keeping a record of that conversation.

Christians have written their prayers down for a very long time. Many of the psalms are written prayers, full of praise, questions, sorrow and thanks. A prayer journal follows that same idea in a personal, everyday way.

What goes in a prayer journal

There is no required list, but most prayer journals include some of these parts:

Prayer journal benefits

People keep prayer journals for many reasons. Here are seven of the most common benefits.

  1. Better focus. Writing slows your thoughts down so your mind does not wander as much while you pray.
  2. More honest prayers. Seeing your words on a page helps you say what you really mean.
  3. A record of answered prayer. Months later you can look back and see what God did. That builds faith for today.
  4. Less worry. Writing a worry down and handing it to God can help it loosen its grip. Research on expressive writing has found that putting feelings into words can help some people process stress.
  5. Faithfulness to others. A prayer list helps you keep your promise to pray for people.
  6. Spiritual growth you can see. Old entries show how God has changed your heart over time.
  7. A legacy. Some people pass their prayer journals on to children or grandchildren as a record of faith.

Who a prayer journal is for

A prayer journal is for anyone who wants to pray more often or more honestly. You do not need to be a strong writer or a long time Christian. If you can write a text message, you can keep a prayer journal.

  • People whose minds wander when they pray. Writing gives your thoughts a track to run on.
  • Worriers. Putting fears on paper and handing them to God can bring real peace.
  • Busy parents. A short page keeps your family's needs in one place. See the prayer journal for moms.
  • New believers. A journal helps you learn to talk to God in your own words.
  • People in a hard season. Grief, illness or waiting are easier to bring to God when you can write it out.
  • Kids and teens. Simple prompts help young people build a habit early. Try the prayer journal for kids.

Common myths about prayer journals

  • You have to write every day. No. Many people write a few times a week. Regular matters more than daily.
  • It has to look pretty. No. A plain notebook and quick handwriting work just as well as art and stickers.
  • You need long, polished prayers. No. One honest sentence is a real prayer.
  • It replaces praying out loud. No. Most people who journal still pray in their own words throughout the day. The journal is one tool, not the only one.
  • It is only for women. No. Men, teens and kids keep prayer journals too. See the prayer journal for men.

Types of prayer journals and how to begin

  • Daily prayer journal. A short dated entry each day.
  • Guided prayer journal. Each page has a verse and prompt, often for a set number of weeks. The Prayer Journal for Women Android app is one example, with 52 weeks of verses, reflection and prayer. It is coming soon on Google Play.
  • Prayer list journal. Mostly names and needs, with answers added over time.
  • ACTS or SOAP journal. Pages follow a set method. See the ACTS prayer journal.
  • Journal for a person. Prayers for one person, like a husband or children.
  • Letters to God. No structure, just honest letters.

Starting is simple: pick a notebook or a page online, choose a time each day, read a short passage, and write a dated prayer. Leave space for answers. If you want a step by step guide, read how to start a prayer journal. If you want ideas for what to write, try our prayer journal prompts.

A prayer journal does not make your prayers more holy or more likely to be heard. God hears every prayer, written or not. A journal is simply a tool to help you pray with focus and remember His faithfulness.

Questions people ask

What is the purpose of a prayer journal?

The purpose of a prayer journal is to help you pray with focus and honesty, and to keep a record of your conversations with God. Over time it shows how He has answered, which builds your faith. It also helps you keep track of the people and needs you have promised to pray for.

Is a prayer journal biblical?

The Bible does not command keeping a prayer journal, but it is full of written prayers, especially in the Psalms. Scripture also encourages us to remember what God has done and to pray about everything. A prayer journal is one practical way to do both.

What is the difference between a prayer journal and a diary?

A diary records what happened in your day and is usually written to yourself. A prayer journal is written to God and includes praise, thanks, confession and requests. Many people combine them by ending a diary entry with a short prayer.

What are the benefits of keeping a prayer journal?

Common benefits include better focus during prayer, more honest prayers, less worry, a record of answered prayer, faithfulness in praying for others, and a clear picture of how you have grown spiritually. Some people also keep their journals as a legacy for their family.

Can a prayer journal be digital?

Yes. You can keep a prayer journal in an app, a document, or a free online page like the ones on this site. Digital journals are easy to carry and search. Paper journals can feel more personal and have fewer distractions. Choose the one you will use.
