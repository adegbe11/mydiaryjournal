An online diary app with nothing to install

Most people looking for a diary app expect to visit an app store. This one is a web app instead. You open the page, and the diary is already there. That means:

  • No download. It is not in any app store. Nothing is installed, and it takes no space on your phone.
  • No sign up. No email, no password, no profile.
  • No ads. There are no ads on the diary page.
  • Works on any device with a browser. Phone, tablet, laptop or desktop.

Each device keeps its own pages. There is no account, so nothing syncs between your phone and your computer. Pick the device you write on most.

How to add the diary app to your home screen

You can make this page open like an app with one tap. Open it in your browser, then follow the steps for your device.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dot menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app), then Add.

Diary app for Windows and Mac

  • Windows: In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar, or open the browser menu and choose to install the app. It gets its own window and icon.
  • Mac: In Safari, use File, then Add to Dock on recent versions. In Chrome, use the install option in the address bar or menu. A bookmark works everywhere.

It installs as My Diary Journal with its own icon and opens in its own window, without the browser bars. Your pages from that browser are all there in My pages.

Diary apps free vs paid

Free diary apps often make money from ads, from a paid upgrade, or from data. Paid apps usually add cloud backup, photos, search and sync. Neither is wrong. Ask yourself what you need.

  • Need just a page and a pen? A free diary app like this one is enough.
  • Need photos, voice notes and sync on five devices? A paid app with an account may be worth it.
  • Need it for a child? Simple and ad free matters more than features. See diary for kids.

How this diary app works

  1. WriteTap the page at the top and start. Add a title, or keep Dear diary. Today's date is filled in for you.
  2. It saves itselfA moment after you stop typing, the page says Saved. There is no save button to forget.
  3. New page each dayTap New page for a fresh, dated entry.

Tips for keeping a diary on your phone

  1. Put the icon on your first home screen. If you see it, you will use it.
  2. Write at the same moment each day. On the bus home, in bed, or with your morning coffee.
  3. Keep it short. Three lines a day beats one long page a month. Try a one line a day journal if you are very busy.
  4. Use a prompt when you are stuck. Browse diary ideas or diary entry topics.
  5. Do not write in private browsing. Those pages vanish when you close the tab.
  6. Save a PDF once a month. It is your backup, and a nice way to look back.

Once the habit sticks, look back. Open My pages on a quiet Sunday and read a few weeks at once. Most people are surprised by how much they had forgotten, and how much has changed. That moment is the real reward of keeping a diary.

Questions people ask

Is there a free diary app with no ads?

Yes. This diary app is free and has no ads inside the diary. It runs in your browser, so you do not download anything or make an account. Write, lock it with a PIN, and print or save pages as PDFs. Your pages are kept on your own device.

Do I need to download this diary app?

No. It is a web app, so it is not in any app store. Open the page in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox and start writing. To use it like an app, add it to your home screen from the browser's Share or menu button. It gets its own icon.

Is there a diary app for Windows or Mac?

This diary works on Windows and Mac in any modern browser. Open the page in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. To open it like an app, install it from Chrome or Edge, or add it to the Dock from Safari on a Mac. Pages written on your computer stay on that computer.

Does the diary app sync between my phone and computer?

No. There is no account, so nothing is stored on a server and nothing syncs. Each browser on each device keeps its own pages. If you want everything in one place, choose one device to write on, and save PDFs to back up pages.

What is the most private diary app?

The most private choices keep your pages on your device, need no account, and are honest about their lock. This diary does all three: pages stay in your browser, there is no sign up, and the PIN lock is clearly a privacy screen, not encryption. Locking your device adds more protection.

Can I add photos to this diary app?

No. This diary is for writing only, so you cannot attach photos, voice notes or stickers. That keeps it simple and keeps everything stored in your browser. If you want photos in your diary, print your pages and add pictures by hand, or use an app built for photo journaling.
