How to choose a diary entry topic

If your teacher gives you a topic, use that one. If you can choose, pick a topic where something happened and you felt something about it. Those make the strongest entries, because a diary entry is about your experience and your emotions.

  • Pick something real if you can. Real memories give you real details.
  • Pick one event, not a whole week.
  • Pick a clear feeling: proud, scared, embarrassed, grateful, guilty, excited.
  • Check the format. Date, day and time, Dear Diary, body, closing, signature. See the diary entry format.

Imaginary topics still use the normal format. Write as if it really happened to you, with a date, Dear Diary, your feelings and a closing thought.

Tips for writing on any diary entry topic

  1. Plan for one minute. Jot down what happened, two details and the main feeling.
  2. Open with your mood. "I have never felt so nervous in my life."
  3. Tell it in order using the past tense.
  4. Show feelings with specific words and small details.
  5. End with a thought or a plan for the future.
  6. Check the format and word count before you finish.

Questions people ask

What are some good diary entry topics?

Good topics are single events with a strong feeling, such as winning a competition, a school trip, a rainy day, a birthday, a fight with a friend, exam results, helping someone in need or the first day at a new school. They give you something to describe and something to reflect on.

What are diary entry topics for class 6?

Common class 6 topics include winning a quiz, a school picnic, sports day, your first day at a new school, a visit to a historical place, planting a tree, a family wedding and your feelings on the last day of vacation. Aim for about 100 to 150 words.

How do I write a diary entry on any topic?

Pick an event with a clear feeling. Write the date, day and time, then Dear Diary. Open with your mood, tell what happened in the past tense, describe how you felt and why, and end with a closing thought and your name. Keep to the word limit.

Can a diary entry be imaginary?

Yes. Teachers sometimes ask for imaginary diary entries, like a day on the moon or a day as your teacher. Use the normal format and write as if it really happened, with feelings and details, so it reads like a real diary entry.

How long should a diary entry be for school?

Younger children often write five to eight sentences. Middle school students usually write around 100 to 150 words, and older students 150 to 200. These are typical ranges, so always follow the word limit in your question or from your teacher.
