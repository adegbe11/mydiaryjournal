A free journal app with no download

This is a web app. Instead of installing it from an app store, you open it in Safari, Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The journal page is ready the moment it loads.

  • Free. No trial, no paywall, no premium plan.
  • No account. No email or password. You start writing right away.
  • Private by design. Entries are saved in your browser on this device, not on our servers.
  • Lock. A PIN keeps the journal closed on a shared computer. It is a privacy screen, not encryption.
  • Keep your pages. Print an entry, or choose Save as PDF to keep a file.
  • Add it to your home screen. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome on Android, open the menu and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. It gets its own icon.

There is no syncing between devices. A journal you keep on your phone stays on your phone. Choose one device for your journal if you want every entry in one place.

What it does not do

To be clear: there are no photos, voice notes, search, reminders or sync here. You get a calm page that saves as you type, a list of your pages with Delete, a PIN lock, prompts, and Print or Save as PDF. For many people that is exactly enough.

Journaling app tips that help you stick with it

  1. Make it tiny. Promise yourself two sentences. You will often write more.
  2. Attach it to a habit. Journal right after brushing your teeth or after your first coffee.
  3. Same device every time. Your entries stay together and the habit feels steady.
  4. Skip the perfect words. Nobody reads this but you.
  5. Back up monthly. Save your favorite entries as PDFs.
  6. Be kind about missed days. Just write today. Do not try to catch up.

One more tip: decide what to do with old entries. Some people keep everything. Others save a monthly PDF and then delete the pages they do not need, so My pages stays short and easy to scroll. Both are fine. What matters is that you feel safe writing honestly, knowing you choose what stays.

Questions people ask

What is the best free journal app?

The best free journal app is one you will actually open, that keeps your writing private, and that lets you keep a copy. This one is free with no account or ads, saves entries on your device, has a PIN lock and lets you print or save PDFs. If you need sync across devices, choose an app with an account instead.

Is there a journal app with no sign up?

Yes. This journal app needs no sign up, email or password. Open the page and start writing. Your entries are saved in your browser on this device. Because there is no account, nothing syncs to other devices, so keep your journal on the device you use most.

Can I use this journal app on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It works in Safari on iPhone and iPad and in Chrome or other browsers on Android. It is not in an app store. To open it like an app, add it to your home screen from the browser's Share or menu button, and it gets its own icon. Each device keeps its own entries.

Are journaling apps private?

Some are more private than others. Apps with accounts store your writing on their servers, so read their privacy policy. This journal app keeps entries in your browser, and we cannot see them. The PIN lock hides the journal on shared devices but is not encryption, so lock your device too.

Is there a journal app I can use on my computer?

Yes. This journal app works in any modern browser on Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook. Open the page in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox and write. In Chrome or Edge you can install it from the address bar so it opens in its own window with an icon. Entries stay on that computer.

Is journaling on an app as good as paper?

Both work. Paper can feel slower and more personal, and some people remember more when they write by hand. An app is faster, always with you, and easy to keep up with. The best one is the one you use most often. You can also print app entries to keep on paper.

Can I delete entries in this journal app?

Yes. Tap My pages and press Delete next to the entry. You will be asked to confirm, and the entry is then removed from this browser for good. If you might want it later, print it or save it as a PDF before you delete it.
