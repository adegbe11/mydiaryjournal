Diary entry: the best day of my life (6 examples)

A diary entry about the best day of your life starts with the date and day, opens with Dear Diary, tells what happened in the past tense, builds up to the big moment, and explains why it meant so much to you. It ends with a closing and your name. Below are 6 complete model entries with different stories and lengths, plus phrases, steps and a page to write your own.

Format for a best day of my life diary entry

This topic is all about one big moment. The best entries build up to it slowly, so the reader feels the excitement too, and then explain why it mattered. Use the standard diary entry format:

  • Date, day and time at the top.
  • Dear Diary, on its own line.
  • A strong first line that tells the diary today was special.
  • The build up: what happened before the big moment, in the past tense.
  • The big moment: slow down here and describe it with your senses.
  • Why it mattered and how you feel now.
  • Closing and name.

You do not need a huge event. A small day can be the best one if you explain clearly why it meant so much to you.

About 80 words with one clear happy moment. Good for grades 2 to 5.

Words and phrases for the best day of your life

Big happy feelings

  • overjoyed, ecstatic, thrilled, delighted, on top of the world
  • proud, grateful, amazed, speechless, emotional
  • relieved, peaceful, complete, brave

Build up words

  • nervous, restless, my heart was pounding, I could hardly breathe
  • the minutes crawled by, I held my breath, everything went quiet

Ready phrases

  • I knew from the moment I woke up that today would be different.
  • I will remember this moment for the rest of my life.
  • I was so happy that I could not stop smiling.
  • Tears of joy rolled down my cheeks.
  • It felt like a dream, and I kept pinching myself.
  • All the hard work was finally worth it.
  • Today was truly the best day of my life.

Use one or two of these, then let your own details do the rest. Need a different topic? Browse our diary entry topics.

How to write your own best day of my life diary entry

  1. Choose your dayA win, a new family member, a trip, meeting someone, doing something brave. Pick the one you remember most clearly.
  2. Write the date, day and time, then Dear DiaryEach on its own line at the top.
  3. Open with a strong lineTell the diary straight away that today was the best day of your life, and hint at why.
  4. Build up to the big momentShare what happened before, including any worries. This makes the moment feel bigger.
  5. Slow down at the big momentWhat did you see, hear and feel? Use two or three sense details.
  6. Explain why it matteredThen close with a closing like Good night and your name.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Rushing the big moment. Students often spend most of the words on breakfast. Spend them on the moment that matters.
  • Not saying why. "It was the best day" is not enough. Tell the diary why it meant so much.
  • Writing about a whole week. A diary entry is about one day.
  • Using the present tense for events. Write I ran, not I run.
  • Too many exclamation marks. One or two are fine. Let your words carry the excitement.
  • Forgetting the date, closing or name.

Questions people ask

How do I start a diary entry about the best day of my life?

Write the date, day and time, then Dear Diary. Make the first line strong and clear, for example: Today was the best day of my life, because I finally met my baby sister. Telling the diary the big news at the start makes the reader want to know how it happened.

What can I write about for the best day of my life?

Any day that meant a lot to you: winning a prize, getting a pet, a new baby in the family, a trip, seeing someone you missed, performing on stage, passing a test, or doing something brave. It does not have to be huge. What matters is that you explain why it was special to you.

How long should a best day of my life diary entry be?

Follow the word limit in your question. Most class 6 to 10 exams ask for 100 to 150 words. Young children can write 60 to 100 words. Creative writing tasks without a limit work well at 200 to 300 words, which leaves room to build up to the big moment.

Can I make up a best day for my diary entry exam?

Yes. In an exam, the examiner is checking your format, language, order of events and expression of feelings, not whether the story really happened. Still, a story based on something real is usually easier to write well, because you remember the small details that make it believable.

How do I end a diary entry about the best day of my life?

End with why the day mattered and how you feel now, then a short closing and your name. For example: I will remember the sound of that applause forever. Good night, Sana. Avoid formal letter endings like Yours sincerely, since a diary is personal.
