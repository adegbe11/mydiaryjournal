Aesthetic journal ideas: 30 pretty pages you can make

An aesthetic journal is one where the look matters as much as the words: a calm color palette, neat handwriting, a little collage and plenty of white space. Below are 30 aesthetic journal ideas in four styles, minimal, vintage, cute and kawaii, and soft nature, each described so you can copy the layout without a picture.

What makes a journal look aesthetic

Most pretty journal pages follow the same few rules. Once you know them, any page can look calm and put together.

  • Pick two or three colors. A small palette looks planned. Too many colors looks busy.
  • Leave white space. Empty space around a title or photo makes it stand out.
  • Use one focal point. One photo, one big title or one drawing per page. Everything else stays small.
  • Mix textures. A strip of washi tape, a scrap of paper and neat handwriting look richer together than any one alone.
  • Keep lines straight. Use a ruler or the dots in your notebook as a guide.

Basic supplies

  • A dotted or plain notebook, A5 size is common
  • A black fine liner pen (0.3 or 0.5)
  • Two or three brush pens or highlighters in muted colors
  • Washi tape in your palette
  • A glue stick and small scissors
  • Printed photos, stickers or magazine cutouts
  • A ruler and a pencil for light guide lines

You do not need everything at once. A black pen, one highlighter and a roll of washi tape are enough for every idea on this page.

Minimal aesthetic journal ideas

Minimal pages use black ink, one accent color and lots of space. They are quick to make and easy to keep up.

  • 1. One line a day. Write the date small in the top left corner and one sentence under it. Leave the rest of the page empty for the week ahead.
  • 2. Big date, small words. Write today's date large in thin capital letters across the top, then three short lines below it.
  • 3. Single color mood grid. Draw a grid of 31 small squares. Shade each day light, medium or dark in one color for your mood.
  • 4. Clean weekly list. Seven day names down the left in small caps, a thin line under each, and one task per day.
  • 5. Black and white photo page. Print one photo in black and white, tape it in the middle, and write a caption underneath in tiny letters.
  • 6. Minimal habit dots. A row of dots for each habit. Fill a dot when you do it. No boxes, no borders.
  • 7. Quote and space. Write one short line in the center of an empty page and leave the rest blank.

Journal ideas vintage style

Vintage pages feel old and cozy: brown and cream tones, old paper, stamps and handwriting that looks like a letter.

Materials

  • Coffee or tea for staining paper
  • Old book pages, maps, sheet music or envelopes
  • Brown, cream and dusty rose paper scraps
  • A brown ink pad or a brown pen
  • Twine, lace or ribbon
  • Old stamps, tickets or postcards

How to make an aged paper background

  1. Brew strong coffee or teaLet it cool a little. Darker coffee gives darker paper.
  2. Dab it onUse a sponge or paper towel to dab it onto plain paper. Darker around the edges looks best.
  3. Let it dry flatPut it on a baking tray or towel. Press it under a heavy book once dry to flatten it.
  4. Tear the edgesTear instead of cutting for a softer, older look. Glue it into your journal as a base.
  • 8. Letter page. Glue an envelope to the page. Write a letter to someone, fold it and tuck it inside.
  • 9. Old map spread. Use a map scrap as the background and write a travel memory or dream trip on top.
  • 10. Library card log. Draw a box that looks like an old library card and log the books you read this month.
  • 11. Pressed flower and lace. Tape a pressed flower over a strip of lace with the date written in brown ink.
  • 12. Ticket collage. Overlap tickets and receipts from one day in a loose pile and tie the corner with twine.
  • 13. Typewriter style. Write in small, neat print capital letters, as if typed, on stained paper.
  • 14. Sepia memory. Print an old family photo in brown tones and write what you know about that day.

Journal ideas cute and kawaii

Kawaii is the Japanese word for cute. These pages use pastel colors, round shapes, little faces on everyday things and lots of stickers.

Materials

  • Pastel highlighters or gel pens (pink, mint, lavender, baby blue)
  • Cute stickers
  • A black pen for faces
  • Washi tape with tiny prints

How to draw a kawaii face

  1. Draw the shapeDraw a simple object, like a cloud, a cup or a cookie.
  2. Add two dot eyesPlace them low and wide apart. This is the secret to the cute look.
  3. Add a tiny smileA small U shape between the eyes.
  4. Add blushTwo small pink ovals under the eyes.
  • 15. Happy food log. Draw what you ate today as tiny smiling foods in a row.
  • 16. Cloud mood tracker. Draw 31 small clouds. Color each one for your mood and give it a face to match.
  • 17. Pastel weekly spread. Seven rounded boxes, each in a different pastel color, with a sticker in the corner.
  • 18. Cute to do list. Draw a small checkbox shaped like a heart or a star for each task.
  • 19. Pet page. Draw your pet or a dream pet in kawaii style and list its favorite things.
  • 20. Sticker diary. Pick one sticker that sums up the day and write two lines beside it.
  • 21. Snack ranking. Rank your top five snacks with little drawings and stars.
  • 22. Cozy night page. A moon with a sleepy face at the top, and three things that made today good below it.

Soft nature and seasonal aesthetic pages

  • 23. Leaf tracker. Draw a branch with 31 leaves. Color one each day in autumn colors.
  • 24. Garden plan. Sketch a small garden plot or windowsill and label what you will plant.
  • 25. Seasonal palette. Paint or color four swatches that match this season and list what you love about it.
  • 26. Moon phases. Draw the moon phases across the top and write a short goal under each new moon.
  • 27. Rain page. Draw falling raindrops down the side and fill the space with what you do on rainy days.
  • 28. Walk log. After a walk, write what you saw, heard and smelled, and tape in a leaf or petal.
  • 29. Sunset colors. Color stripes from orange to purple and write about the best evening this month.
  • 30. Season goals. Divide the page into four corners for spring, summer, fall and winter goals.

Tips for a pretty journal without the stress

  • Sketch the layout in pencil first, then ink it and erase the pencil.
  • If you make a mistake, cover it with washi tape or a sticker and keep going.
  • Practice titles on scrap paper before writing them in your journal.
  • Do not compare your pages to photos online. Many of those took hours.
  • Remember the words still matter most. A plain page you wrote is worth more than a pretty page you left empty.

Questions people ask

How do I make my journal look aesthetic?

Pick two or three colors and stick to them, leave white space, and give each page one focal point such as a photo or a big title. Add texture with washi tape or a paper scrap. Neat handwriting and straight lines do more than fancy supplies.

What do I need for an aesthetic journal?

A dotted notebook, a black fine liner pen, two or three muted markers or highlighters, washi tape and a glue stick are enough. Add stickers, printed photos and paper scraps later if you enjoy it.

What is a vintage journal?

A vintage journal uses an old, cozy look: coffee stained paper, brown and cream colors, old book pages, stamps, tickets, lace and twine. Many people make them as junk journals from scraps they already have. See our junk journal ideas for more.

What does kawaii mean in journaling?

Kawaii is a Japanese word for cute. In journaling it means pastel colors, rounded shapes, cute stickers, and little faces drawn on everyday things like clouds, food and plants. The trick is two dot eyes placed low and wide, a tiny smile and pink cheeks.

What should I write in an aesthetic journal?

The same things you would write in any journal: your day, a gratitude list, goals, memories or a favorite quote. The aesthetic is the style around the words. If you need ideas, try our journal prompts.
