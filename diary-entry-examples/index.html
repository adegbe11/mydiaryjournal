What is a diary entry?

A diary entry is one page or one section of a diary, written about a single day or event. It usually has the date at the top, often starts with Dear diary, tells what happened in the past tense, and shares the writer's feelings. It is informal and personal, because it is written for yourself.

Every example below follows that shape. Some are written for school, with the date, day and time and a signature. Others are personal and looser. Both are correct. For the exact school layout, see the diary entry format.

What these diary entry examples have in common

  • A date at the top, sometimes with the day and time.
  • First person, using I, me and my.
  • Past tense for what happened, and present tense for feelings now.
  • One main event instead of everything that happened.
  • Specific details, like oranges rolling, pancakes shaped like numbers, or the smell of wet earth.
  • Feelings, said plainly: scared, proud, jealous, lucky.
  • A closing thought, often a plan or something to remember.

Questions people ask

What is a diary entry?

A diary entry is a short, personal piece of writing about one day or event, written in the first person and dated at the top. It usually begins with Dear diary, tells what happened in the past tense and shares how the writer felt. It is meant to be private and informal.

What is a good example of a diary entry?

A good diary entry has a date, focuses on one event, includes real details, and says how you felt. For example, an entry about a fight with a friend that explains what happened, admits your own part in it and ends with a plan to say sorry is a strong example.

How do you write a short diary entry?

Write the date, then three lines: what happened, how you felt, and one thing to remember. That is a full diary entry. Short entries are great for busy days and help you keep the habit of writing every day. You can always add more on a day when you have time.

What should a diary entry include for school?

For school, include the date, day and time at the top, the salutation Dear Diary, a body in the first person past tense describing the event and your feelings, a closing thought, and your name as a signature. Keep to the word limit in the question.

Can a diary entry be about anything?

Yes. A personal diary entry can be about any event, feeling, person or thought. School questions usually give you a topic, such as a birthday, a rainy day or a school trip, and you should stay on that topic. If you can choose, pick an event with a clear feeling.
