How to use these diary ideas

Pick one idea that feels fun or that you have a feeling about. Write a few lines, or a whole page. There are no wrong answers and nobody is checking your spelling.

Your diary is yours. Write the way you talk. If an idea does not fit today, skip it and pick another.

Today in emojis

Describe your day with three emojis, then explain each one.

My top five

Top five songs, snacks or shows right now.

Dear future me

A letter to open in one year.

Easy diary ideas for every day

  1. The best part of today and the not so great part.
  2. What you wore today and why you picked it.
  3. Something that made you giggle today.
  4. What you had for lunch. Rate it out of ten.
  5. A song stuck in your head right now.
  6. Something new you learned today, at school or anywhere.
  7. Your mood today as a weather report: sunny, cloudy, stormy?
  8. Something kind you did today, or something kind someone did for you.
  9. What you are looking forward to tomorrow.
  10. Three things you can see from where you are sitting right now.
  11. The last photo on your camera roll and the story behind it.
  12. If today had a title like a book, what would it be?

Diary ideas about friends and family

  1. How you met your best friend. Tell the whole story.
  2. Your favorite memory with your best friend so far.
  3. Something you and your friends always laugh about.
  4. A time a friend made you feel really cared for.
  5. A small fight with a friend. What happened and how did you fix it?
  6. Your mom, dad or the person who looks after you: one thing you love about them.
  7. A family tradition you love, like a holiday, a meal or a movie night.
  8. A brother, sister or cousin who drives you crazy, and one nice thing about them.
  9. Your grandparents or an older relative. What stories do they tell?
  10. What makes a good friend? Make a list.
  11. A friend you miss and what you would do together if you saw them today.
  12. A person you would like to get to know better.

All about me diary ideas

  1. An all about me page: name, age, favorite color, food, animal, song and show.
  2. Three things you love about yourself.
  3. Something you are really good at, and how you got good at it.
  4. Something you are still learning and want to get better at.
  5. Your room right now. What would you change if you could?
  6. Your perfect weekend, from waking up to bedtime.
  7. Your comfort things: the snack, show, outfit and place that make you feel better.
  8. A time you were brave.
  9. What makes you different from everyone else in your class.
  10. Your favorite thing to do when you are alone.
  11. Something you wish people knew about you.
  12. Your style in five words.

Diary ideas about feelings

Writing your feelings down can help them feel smaller and clearer. If something is really worrying you, it is also a good idea to talk to a parent, teacher or another adult you trust.

  1. Something that made you happy this week, big or small.
  2. A time you felt left out. What would have helped?
  3. Something you are nervous about right now. Then write the best thing that could happen.
  4. A time you felt really proud of yourself.
  5. What you do when you feel sad. What helps the most?
  6. Something you wish you had said to someone.
  7. A moment you felt embarrassed that is funny now.
  8. What calm feels like to you. Where are you, and what can you hear?
  9. A worry you can let go of today.
  10. Five things you are thankful for right now.

Dreams and goals

  1. What you want to be when you grow up, and three jobs you would also love to try.
  2. A place in the world you dream of visiting. What would you do first?
  3. A goal for this month and the first small step.
  4. Something you want to learn, like an instrument, a language or a sport.
  5. Your dream bedroom. Describe every corner.
  6. A letter to yourself to open on your next birthday.
  7. If you could start any club at school, what would it be?
  8. Ten things on your bucket list.
  9. A woman or girl you look up to, real or from a book, and why.
  10. What you hope people say about you when you are older.

Fun and silly diary ideas

  1. If you had a magic power for one day, what would you do with it?
  2. Invent a new ice cream flavor and describe it.
  3. If your pet could talk, what would it say about you?
  4. Design your dream outfit for a party.
  5. Write a story that starts with "The door was open just a crack..."
  6. If you could swap lives with a character from a book or movie for one day, who would it be?
  7. Your dream sleepover. Who is there, what do you eat, what do you watch?
  8. A day where everything is upside down. What happens?
  9. Make up a new holiday and plan how to celebrate it.
  10. Would you rather live by the ocean or in the mountains? Why?
  11. Write a short poem about your favorite season.
  12. If you opened a shop, what would it sell?

Aesthetic diary ideas for your pages

Aesthetic diary pages are about how the page looks as much as what it says. Try these when you want to get creative. For more looks, see aesthetic journal ideas.

  1. A mood tracker for the month: one small square or heart for each day, colored by mood.
  2. A color palette page: five colors you love right now with a word for each.
  3. A favorite quote written big in your best handwriting, with doodles around it.
  4. A "currently" page: reading, watching, listening to, loving, wishing.
  5. A seasonal page with pressed leaves, flowers or drawings from outside.
  6. A ticket and wrapper page: stick in little things from a fun day out.
  7. A playlist page drawn like an album cover.
  8. A doodle of your desk or bedside table.
  9. A vision page with cut out words and pictures of things you want.
  10. A gratitude jar drawing, with each thing you are thankful for on a paper star.
  11. A week at a glance page with a cute header and a small drawing for each day.
  12. A sky page: draw or describe the sky every evening for a week.
  13. A "things that made me smile" page with washi tape borders.
  14. A friendship page with your friends' handwriting and their favorite things.
  15. A cozy page about your favorite rainy day activities.
  16. A memory page from your last trip or holiday, with a small map you draw yourself.
  17. A page of doodled flowers, hearts and stars to use when you do not want to write words.

Tips for keeping your diary private and fun

  • Keep it simple. A few lines a day is enough. You do not have to fill a page.
  • Make it yours. Use stickers, colored pens and drawings in a paper diary. Online, try a fun title for each page.
  • Keep it private. A diary with a lock, a hidden spot, or an online diary with a PIN helps. Read about a secret diary for ideas.
  • Stay safe online. Do not post diary pages with your full name, school or address. Your diary is just for you.
  • Be kind to yourself. Write about mistakes like you would talk to your best friend.

Questions people ask

What should a girl write in her diary?

Anything that is on her mind: what happened today, friends, family, school, feelings, dreams, favorite things and fun lists. A diary is a private space, so there are no rules. Many girls like to mix writing with drawings, stickers and quotes to make the pages feel special.

What are some easy diary ideas?

Easy diary ideas include the best part of your day, what you ate for lunch, a song stuck in your head, your mood as a weather report, three things you are thankful for, and a top five list of your favorite things right now. Each one takes just a few minutes.

How do I make my diary aesthetic?

Pick a small color palette and stick with it, use one or two nice pens, and add washi tape, stickers or doodles. Try page ideas like a mood tracker, a currently page, a quote page or a playlist page. Leave some white space so the page feels calm.

How can I keep my diary private?

Use a diary with a lock or keep it somewhere only you know about. An online diary with a PIN works too on a shared computer. Most of all, ask your family to respect that your diary is private, and do not share pages online.

What age is good to start a diary?

Any age that you can write a few sentences. Many girls start around 7 or 8 years old, often with a diary gift. Younger children can draw pictures and write one line. There is no age that is too late to start.
