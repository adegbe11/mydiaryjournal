How to keep a daily diary

A daily diary works best when it is small enough to do every day. Five minutes is plenty.

  1. Pick your timeMost people write at night, when the day is complete. Others write the next morning with coffee. Pick one and keep it.
  2. Write the dateThe diary above adds it for you. Dates are what make a diary a diary.
  3. Tell the day in orderMorning, afternoon, evening. Two or three sentences each is enough.
  4. Add one feelingHow did today feel overall? One word or one line.
  5. Save one detailA smell, a joke, a sentence someone said. Details are what you will love rereading.

Missed a day? Do not go back and fill it in. Just write today. A daily diary with gaps is still a diary.

Daily diary vs daily diary journal

A daily diary records what happened. A daily journal explores what you think. A daily diary journal does a little of both: a few lines about the day, then one question to reflect on.

Daily diaryDaily diary journal
FocusEvents of the dayEvents plus thoughts
Length3 to 8 linesHalf a page
Ends withA feeling or detailA question or lesson
Best forRemembering your lifeRemembering and growing

Tips to write a daily diary every day

  1. Make it tiny. On busy days, write one line. Try a one line a day journal.
  2. Link it to a habit. Write right after brushing your teeth at night.
  3. Keep it where you see it. Put the diary on your phone's home screen.
  4. Write facts first. Facts are easy. Feelings often follow on their own.
  5. Reread on Sundays. Looking back at the week makes you want to keep going.
  6. Back up monthly. Save a PDF of each month's best pages.

Questions people ask

What do you write in a daily diary?

Write the date, what happened in the morning, afternoon and evening, how you felt, and one detail you want to remember. A daily diary does not need to be long. Three to eight lines is plenty for most days, and a single line is fine on busy ones.

Can I keep a daily diary online for free?

Yes. This page is a free daily diary online with no sign up or account. Write today's page above and tap New page tomorrow. Pages save in this browser on this device, and you can lock the diary with a PIN or print pages and save them as PDFs.

How long should a daily diary entry be?

As long as you like, but short is easier to keep up. Most daily diary entries are three to eight sentences. Writing a little every day is better than writing a lot once in a while. If you only have a minute, one good sentence is enough.

When is the best time to write a daily diary?

At night is most common, because the day is finished and you can see it all. Some people prefer the next morning, when they feel calmer. The best time is the one you can keep, so tie it to something you already do every day.

What if I miss a day in my daily diary?

Just write today. There is no need to catch up or feel bad. Gaps are normal, and a diary with gaps still holds a lot of memories. If you miss often, make your entries smaller so they fit into even your busiest days.

What is a daily diary used for?

A daily diary is used to remember your life, notice how you feel over time, and clear your head at the end of the day. Some people also use one to track sleep, meals, health or work. Reading back months later shows you patterns and memories you would otherwise forget.
