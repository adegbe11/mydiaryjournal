A free private diary for women

The diary at the top of this page is free and private. Open it, write, and close the tab. It saves as you type.

  • No sign up and no account. No email list, no password to remember.
  • Your pages stay with you. They are saved in this browser on this device and are not sent to us.
  • PIN lock to keep it closed on a shared family computer. It keeps pages out of sight, but it is not encryption.
  • Print or save as PDF to keep a copy of the pages that matter.
  • Any browser, any device. No app to install. Add it to your phone's home screen and it opens like one.

There is no sync between devices, so pick the one you write on most. Open the full online diary any time.

How to choose a paper diary for women

If you love pen and paper, the right diary is the one that fits how you actually live. Think about these before you buy.

TypeGood forWatch out for
Dated daily diaryA fixed page each day, great for routineBlank pages if you miss days
Undated notebookWriting when you want, at any lengthNo structure on blank days
Five year or one line a day diaryBusy women who want a tiny daily habitVery little space per day
Guided journalPrompts on every pagePrompts may not fit your life
Diary plannerPlans and notes in one bookPlans can crowd out reflection
Lockable diaryPrivacy at homeLocks are more symbolic than secure

Small details that make a big difference

  • Size. A5 fits a handbag and still gives room to write. Larger feels luxurious at home.
  • Paper weight. Thicker paper (around 100 gsm or more) stops ink showing through.
  • Lay flat binding so you can write without holding the page down.
  • Ribbon marker and pocket for today's page and small keepsakes.
  • A cover you like touching. You are more likely to open something that feels good in your hand.

If you want just a few lines a day, a one line a day journal may suit you better than a full page diary.

Kinds of diaries women keep

Your diary does not have to be one thing. Many women mix two or three of these.

  • Daily diary. What happened today and how it felt. See daily diary.
  • Gratitude diary. Three good things a day. See gratitude journal.
  • Prayer journal. Verses, prayers and answered prayers. See prayer journal for women.
  • Pregnancy or baby diary. Notes, firsts and feelings to keep for your child. See pregnancy journal.
  • Health and mood diary. Sleep, energy, cycle, mood and symptoms, useful to bring to a doctor. See mood journal.
  • Travel diary. Places, meals and people from each trip.

Tips for keeping a diary when life is busy

  1. Shrink it. Three lines: what happened, how you felt, what you are grateful for.
  2. Attach it to something you already do. Morning coffee, the school run wait, or right before you turn the light off.
  3. Keep it where you are. A diary on your bedside table or a diary bookmarked on your phone gets used.
  4. Let go of perfect. Messy handwriting and unfinished sentences are fine.
  5. Write the hard days too. Some of the most useful pages to read back are the ones written on bad days.
  6. Read back monthly. You will notice patterns in your energy, mood and relationships.

A diary can be a good place to untangle stress, but it does not replace professional care. If you are struggling for a long time, talk to a doctor or counselor. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services. In the US, call or text 988.

Questions people ask

What is the best diary for women?

The best diary is one that fits your life. If you are busy, a one line a day or five year diary works well. If you like to write longer, an undated notebook with thick paper is ideal. If you want privacy and ease, a free online diary with a PIN works on any device.

What should a woman write in her diary?

Write what happened today and how it felt, what you are grateful for, what is worrying you, and what you hope for. You can also write about relationships, health, work, faith and goals. Prompts help on days when you do not know where to start.

Is there a free private diary for women online?

Yes. The diary on this page is free with no sign up and no account. Pages are saved in the browser on your device and are not sent to us. You can set a PIN to keep it closed on a shared computer, and print any page.

How do I find time to keep a diary?

Make it small and tie it to something you already do, like your morning coffee or getting into bed. Three lines takes two minutes. Keep your diary within reach, and on busy days write one sentence. A short habit is easier to keep than a long one.

Is a diary or a journal better for women?

Neither is better. A diary records what happened each day, and a journal is a place to reflect, plan or pray. Many women do both on the same page: a few lines about the day, then a thought or a prayer. See our diary vs journal guide for more.
