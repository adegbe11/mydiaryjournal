About My Diary Journal

My Diary Journal is a free place to write. It is a private online diary that feels like paper, plus prompts, templates and plain guides to help you keep writing.

On this page
  1. What we believe
  2. How the diary works

What we believe

Writing things down helps people remember, heal, pray and grow. It should be simple, private and free. You should not need an account to write about your own life.

How the diary works

Your pages are saved in your web browser on your own device. We do not have a server that stores your writing, so we cannot read it. You can print any page or save it as a PDF, and lock the diary with a PIN on shared computers.

Our app

We also make Prayer Journal for Women, an Android app with 52 weeks of Bible verses, reflection and prayer.
