How it works

A free online diary that stays private

Most online diaries ask you to make an account, then keep your writing on their servers. This one does not. Your pages are saved in your own web browser on the device you are using, so nothing you write is sent to us. There is no sign up, no email and no password to remember unless you choose a PIN.

  1. Start writingType a title and write on the lines. Each page is dated for you.
  2. It saves as you typeClose the tab and come back later. Your page is still there on this device.
  3. Find old pagesTap My pages to see everything you have written, newest first.
  4. Lock it if you share a computerTap Lock and choose a PIN. The diary asks for it each time it opens.
  5. Keep a copyTap Print to print a page or save it as a PDF.

What to write in your diary

Write what happened, how it made you feel, and one thing you want to remember. Three sentences is a real diary entry. If you get stuck, try our diary ideas, journal prompts or diary entry examples. New to this? Read how to write a diary.

Questions people ask

Is this online diary really free?

Yes. The diary is free to use with no sign up, no account and no ads in the diary.

Is my online diary private?

Your pages are saved in your web browser on your own device. They are not sent to us or stored on a server, so we cannot read them.

Can I put a password on my diary?

Yes. Tap Lock to set a PIN. The diary will ask for it each time it opens on this device. The PIN keeps the diary closed on a shared computer. It is not encryption, so keep your device itself locked too. See private diary with lock.

Can I use my diary on another phone or computer?

Pages stay on the device and browser where you wrote them. There is no syncing, which is part of what keeps them private. Print a page or save it as a PDF to keep a copy anywhere.

What happens if I clear my browser?

Clearing your browser data or using a private window removes saved pages. Print or save a PDF of pages you want to keep for good.
