Why keep a diary

A diary is the simplest way to remember your life. A few lines a day turn into a record of where you were, who you loved and how you grew. Writing things down also helps you calm a busy mind and notice how you really feel.

You do not need a fancy notebook. Our free online diary works on any phone or computer, with no sign up, and keeps your pages on your own device.

Start here

New to keeping a diary? Read how to write a diary, look at a few diary entry examples, then open the online diary and write three sentences about today. Stuck on what to say? Our diary ideas and diary entry topics have hundreds of starting points.
